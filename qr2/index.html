    QR MedCheck - Executive Project Brief

    Problem Statement

    Manual patient and medication verification increases the risk of human errors, especially during busy hospital situations. Healthcare workers experience difficulties confirming patient identities, checking medication details, and ensuring that the correct medication is given to the right patient. These manual processes can also be time-consuming, leading to delays and potential medication errors.

    Description of Proposal

    QR MedCheck is a low-cost, QR-based patient and medication verification system designed to improve safety and efficiency in hospitals. By scanning QR codes assigned to patients and their medications, healthcare workers can quickly verify patient identities and confirm medication details before administration. The system alerts users when mismatches or incorrect information are detected and helps maintain updated medication records.
